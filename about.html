Who We Are

A small local team with a personal approach to travel

Elias Travel and Tour is a personalized travel and visa consultation service, operating locally in Doha, Qatar. We were built on a simple belief: travel should feel personal, not processed.

When you contact us, you're not a ticket number. You talk directly to the people who plan your visas, book your flights, and shape your itinerary — and they stay with you from your first question to your final departure.

Whether you're visiting Qatar for the first time, arranging a work permit, or planning a private tour of the city and the desert, our job is the same: take the stress out of travel and keep the joy in it.

Our Approach

One point of contact, end to end

Most travel problems start with too many intermediaries — a visa agent here, a ticket counter there, a tour desk somewhere else. We remove that friction.

Tell us your dates, your budget and your interests once. We handle the visa questions, compare and book the flights, and build the itinerary around you — then we stay reachable while you travel.
