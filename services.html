Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a visa to visit Qatar?
Many travellers don't — citizens of 90+ countries (including most European, American, Gulf and several Asian nationalities) receive visa-free entry for stays up to 30–90 days. Others can qualify for a visit or e-visa. Send us your nationality and we'll confirm exactly what applies to you, and help with the application where needed.
How does the visa assistance actually work?
We review your documents (passport, photos, invitation or sponsorship letters where applicable), advise on the correct category, and guide you through the submission using our local support in Qatar. You'll always know the status of your file and what happens next.
Can a tour be customized?
Yes — it's the default, not the exception. Tell us which places matter to you, what to skip, and how much time you have. Past requests have included early-morning airport pickups, family-friendly pacing, food stops at local cafés and photo-first itineraries.
I have a short layover in Doha — is that possible?
It's one of the most popular scenarios. We build short city itineraries timed around your flights, meet you at arrivals, and guarantee drop-off well before your departure. Most nationalities can enter visa-free during the layover.
How big can my group be?
Our services cover individuals, families and groups of all sizes. For tours, larger groups are simply handled with additional vehicles — just tell us your numbers when you enquire.
Which languages do you support?
We work in Arabic and English, and we're happy to arrange support in other languages when needed for tours and documentation. Just let us know in advance.
How do I book?
Call us on +974 3997 1400, send us a DM on TikTok @eliastravel_tour, or use the contact form. Say your dates, group size and interest, and you'll get a personal reply with a plan and a quote.
